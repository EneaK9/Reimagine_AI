Request account deletion

ReimagineAI — last updated July 22, 2026

You can request deletion of your ReimagineAI account and associated personal data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email privacy@reimagine.ai from the email address linked to your account.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my ReimagineAI account.
  3. Include your username (if you remember it).

What we delete

After we verify your request, we delete or irreversibly anonymize:

  • Your account credentials (email, username, password hash)
  • Chat conversations and design preferences stored with your account
  • Room photos and generated images associated with your account on our servers

What we may retain

We may keep limited records required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance (for example, short-lived server logs) for a limited period, then remove them according to our retention practices.

Timing

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email when the process is finished.

See also our Privacy Policy.
